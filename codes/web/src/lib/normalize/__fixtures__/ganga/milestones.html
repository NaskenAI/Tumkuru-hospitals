Milestones

Continued Growth and Excellence

As a leading healthcare provider in the region, Ganga Hospital continues to grow, adapt to emerging healthcare trends and uphold its commitment to excellence in patient care, making significant contributions to the health and well-being of the community.

Ganga Clinic to Ganga Hospital - On Timeline

2026
2026
Research and Innovation

With a focus on advancing medical knowledge and improving patient care, Ganga Hospital has a plan to establish research facilities from 2026 and to collaborate with academic institutions to conduct clinical trials and innovative treatment approaches.

2024
2024
Ganga Hospital’s New Building

The construction of Hospital’s New Building was started in the Early’s of 2023 and ended in 2024. It is a significant milestone for Ganga Hospital. With its new, modern facility in place, Ganga Hospital is well-positioned to continue its mission of providing high-quality healthcare services to the community.

2024
Apply for Accreditation and Recognition

In the Early 2024, Ganga Hospital has applied to obtain accreditation from relevant healthcare authorities and to earn  recognition for its high-quality care, patient safety measures and clinical outcomes.

2024
Community Outreach and Education

With the Inauguration of New Ganga Hospital on Feb 2024, it has planned to initiate community outreach programs and health education initiatives to promote preventive healthcare and raise awareness about common medical conditions.

2023
2023
Rebranding as Ganga Hospital

In the Year of 2023, Ganga Clinic evolved into a larger healthcare institution offering a wide range of specialized services, it started a rebranding process to reflect its enhanced capabilities and status as a hospital.

2021
2021
Acquisition of Specialists

From 2021, Ganga Clinic recruited Consultant Specialists and Consultant Surgeons, in various medical fields to provide comprehensive care to patients, including surgeons, oncologists, neurologists and others.

2020
2020
Enhanced Technology and Equipment

During 2020, after Covid-19 era, Ganga Clinic invested in advanced medical technology and equipments for the improvement of diagnostic accuracy and treatment outcomes. Also got Collaborated with Various Hospitals and Diagnostic Centers in Tumkur City.

2019
2019
Increase in Infrastructure

With the increasing demand for spinal healthcare services, Ganga Clinic was expanded its infrastructure during 2019 by adding more consultation rooms, diagnostic facilities and In-House Pharmacy.

2018
2018
Expansion of Services

During 2018, Ganga Clinic was expanded its services to include additional specialties such as cardiology orthopedics and surgery to cater to the growing healthcare needs of the community.

2017
2017
Foundation of Ganga Clinic

Ganga Clinic was established in 2017 as a small healthcare facility, likely focusing on primary care services such as general medicine, pediatrics and obstetrics.

Short Introduction

Ganga Hospital was established in February 2024 by Dr Vijay Tubaki, a practising CONSULTANT SPINE & ORTHOPEDIC SURGEON with great passion in his profession. He is the active member of Asia Pacific Spine Society, Association Spine Surgeons of India, Indian Orthopaedic Association, Karnataka Orthopaedic Association. He brings more than 10 years of Orthocare experience to the company and holds different positions in various organisations.
